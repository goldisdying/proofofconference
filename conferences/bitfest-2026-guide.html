Bitfest 2026: The Complete Guide — Manchester, UK

Bitfest 2026 is a Bitcoin festival taking place November 20–22 at the Pendulum Hotel in Manchester, UK — three days of Bitcoin content, community, and culture in the heart of England's most vibrant northern city.

Tickets and information at bitfest.io.

The Basics

Dates: November 20–22, 2026
Venue: Pendulum Hotel, Manchester, United Kingdom
Format: In-Person, three days

Why Bitfest Stands Out

Bitfest isn't a typical conference — the name signals something more celebratory and community-driven than a standard panels-and-keynotes format. Manchester is the ideal city for that kind of event. The UK's second city has an energy and cultural confidence that makes it a natural home for a Bitcoin gathering that wants to feel like a genuine festival rather than a corporate event.

November in Manchester places Bitfest at the end of the 2026 Bitcoin conference calendar — a fitting close to what has been an extraordinary year for the global Bitcoin community.

Manchester as a Bitcoin City

Manchester has one of the UK's most active Bitcoin communities. The city's history of radical politics, working-class culture, and innovation — from the Industrial Revolution to the music scene that gave the world The Smiths, Oasis, and Joy Division — makes it culturally aligned with Bitcoin's emphasis on decentralization, individual sovereignty, and disrupting established systems.

The Pendulum Hotel is a well-regarded venue in Manchester city centre, close to Manchester Piccadilly station and the city's excellent restaurant and bar scene. November in Manchester is cool and atmospheric — the city's Christmas markets typically begin around this time, adding an extra dimension to any extended stay.

Getting to Manchester

Manchester Airport is one of the UK's busiest, with direct flights from across Europe, North America, the Middle East, and beyond. From the airport, the city centre is accessible by Metrolink tram in about 20 minutes. Manchester Piccadilly station connects to London in just over 2 hours by train, making it an easy add-on to any UK trip.
