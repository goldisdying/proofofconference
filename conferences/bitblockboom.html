About BitBlockBoom

BitBlockBoom is Texas' original Bitcoin conference, founded and organized by Gary Leland. The event has grown into one of the most respected grassroots Bitcoin gatherings in the United States, known for its community-first atmosphere and high-quality speaker lineups.

Past Speakers

Previous editions have featured Jimmy Song, Cory Klippsten, Caitlin Long, Parker Lewis, Pierre Rochard, Lawrence Lepard, Joe Kelly, Brady Swenson, Bob Burnett, Guy Swann, Luke Broyles, and many more. The 2027 speaker lineup will be announced as the event approaches.
