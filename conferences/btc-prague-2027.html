About BTC Prague 2027

BTC Prague is the largest Bitcoin-only conference in Europe. It began during the 2022 bear market and has grown into a three-day event drawing roughly 10,000 attendees from across the continent and beyond.

The 2027 edition moves to an earlier slot on the calendar — May 6–8 rather than the June dates used in previous years — and organizers have confirmed the event is relocating to a new, larger venue in central Prague. Earlier editions were held at PVA EXPO PRAHA.

Programming spans a main stage, an expo stage, and a large exhibition hall. Content ranges from beginner-friendly sessions to deeper industry talks, and the conference is conducted in English, with a separate outdoor stage running Czech-language content for local audiences.
