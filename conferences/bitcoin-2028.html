Based on this history, there are four plausible scenarios for Bitcoin 2028:

Miami. Miami hosted the conference for three consecutive years (2021-2023) and has not hosted since. By 2028, it will have been five years since the last Miami edition. Miami is where the modern era of the Bitcoin Conference began, and a return to South Florida would carry strong nostalgia and brand recognition.

Las Vegas. Las Vegas hosted back-to-back in 2025 and 2026 and has proven venue infrastructure at The Venetian. It is a natural default for large-scale events and could return for 2028.

Nashville. Nashville is hosting in both 2024 and 2027, making it the only city to appear twice in non-consecutive years. The Music City Center has become closely associated with the Bitcoin Conference brand, and a third Nashville edition is not out of the question.

A new city. The conference has grown significantly each year. A move to a new major U.S. city or even an international location would signal continued expansion.

About the Bitcoin Conference

The annual Bitcoin Conference is the world's largest gathering dedicated exclusively to Bitcoin. It brings together developers, investors, miners, companies, and Bitcoin enthusiasts from around the world. Previous editions have featured keynotes from industry leaders, politicians, and cultural figures. Bitcoin 2024 in Nashville made headlines when then-presidential candidate Donald Trump appeared on stage, becoming one of the most talked-about moments in Bitcoin conference history.
