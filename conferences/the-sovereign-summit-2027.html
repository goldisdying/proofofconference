About The Sovereign Summit 2027

The Sovereign Summit is a four-day event at the Fontainebleau Miami Beach built around a single premise: sovereignty at every scale, across AI, energy, money, and beyond. Rather than treating Bitcoin as an isolated subject, the programming places it alongside the other systems that determine how much control individuals and institutions actually hold over their own future.

The format is deliberately small. Attendance is capped at 1,000, with VIP limited to 200 — a fraction of the size of the flagship Bitcoin conferences. Everything happens under one oceanfront roof: the conference, the lodging, the side events, and the parties all take place at the same property, so the hallway conversations continue at the pool and at dinner rather than scattering across a city.

The main conference runs Friday February 5 and Saturday February 6, with a Bitcoin Fundamentals day on Thursday and the Satoshi Farewell Brunch on Sunday available as add-ons. The venue is the Fontainebleau's Coastal Convention Center, on a property that has been a Miami Beach landmark since Morris Lapidus designed it in 1954.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is The Sovereign Summit 2027?

February 4–7, 2027. The main two-day conference runs Friday February 5 and Saturday February 6. Bitcoin Fundamentals is Thursday February 4, and the Satoshi Farewell Brunch is Sunday February 7.

Where is The Sovereign Summit held?

The Fontainebleau Miami Beach, at the Coastal Convention Center. The hotel sits oceanfront at 4441 Collins Avenue in Miami Beach, Florida.

How much are Sovereign Summit tickets?

The Summit Pass is $299 and the VIP Summit Pass is $1,999. Use code FOMO for 10% off. Prices increase in tiers, and increases are announced roughly one week in advance.

What is included with the VIP Summit Pass?

VIP includes everything in the Summit Pass plus premium breakfast and lunch both days, reserved front-row main stage seating, exclusive VIP areas, the VIP Welcome Reception on Thursday, the Oceanfront Soiree on Friday, and included tickets to Bitcoin Fundamentals, the After Party, and the Satoshi Farewell Brunch. VIP is limited to 200 spots.

How many people attend The Sovereign Summit?

Attendance is capped at 1,000 by design, with VIP limited to 200. The cap is intentional — the event is built for depth of conversation rather than scale.

Is The Sovereign Summit a Bitcoin-only conference?

No. Bitcoin is central, but the programming spans AI, energy, freedom, and money. The framing is sovereignty across all of those domains rather than Bitcoin in isolation.

Is there a hotel room block?

Yes. The Sovereign Summit has an exclusive rate at the Fontainebleau, bookable through the link on the official site. Since the conference, lodging, and evening events all happen at the same property, staying on site is worth considering.
