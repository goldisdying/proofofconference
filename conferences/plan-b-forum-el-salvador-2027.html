About Plan ₿ Forum El Salvador 2027

Plan ₿ Forum El Salvador is the leading Bitcoin conference in Central America. It brings together heads of state, technologists, entrepreneurs, and Bitcoin educators around nation-state adoption, economics, financial freedom, and freedom of speech.

The forum is the El Salvador edition of the Plan ₿ series, which also runs an annual event in Lugano, Switzerland. Past El Salvador editions have been held across San Salvador venues including the Sheraton Presidente Hotel, the Museum of Art of El Salvador (MARTE), and Teatro Presidente.

Previous speakers have included Paolo Ardoino of Tether, Adam Back of Blockstream, Elizabeth Stark of Lightning Labs, Samson Mow of Jan3, and Jameson Lopp of Casa. The 2025 edition sold out at roughly 2,500 tickets, about half of them to Salvadorans, and was the first Bitcoin conference in the country to run a stage dedicated entirely to Spanish-language content.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is Plan ₿ Forum El Salvador 2027?

January 29–30, 2027, in San Salvador, El Salvador.

Where is Plan ₿ Forum El Salvador held?

Ticketing lists Museo de Arte de El Salvador (MARTE) in San Salvador for 2027. Past editions have used multiple venues including the Sheraton Presidente Hotel and Teatro Presidente.

How much are Plan ₿ Forum El Salvador tickets?

The Peer ticket tier starts at $99. Higher tiers are available, and prices typically rise as the event approaches.

Is the conference in English or Spanish?

Both. Programming runs in English with live Spanish translation, and recent editions have included a stage dedicated entirely to Spanish-language content.

How many people attend Plan ₿ Forum El Salvador?

The 2025 edition sold out at roughly 2,500 attendees, with about half coming from within El Salvador.

Is there also a Plan ₿ Forum in Lugano?

Yes. The Lugano edition runs separately in Switzerland — the 2026 Lugano forum is scheduled for October 23–24, 2026.
