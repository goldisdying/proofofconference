Best Bitcoin Conferences in Europe 2026

Europe has established itself as the beating heart of the global Bitcoin conference circuit. With events spanning from Dublin to Prague, Madrid to Helsinki, the continent offers more Bitcoin gatherings per capita than anywhere else in the world. This guide covers the must-attend European Bitcoin conferences for 2026 — all confirmed and upcoming as of March 2026.
