Why Hong Kong

Hong Kong has re-established itself as one of Asia's most Bitcoin-forward jurisdictions. The city has moved to create a clear regulatory framework for digital assets, making it a natural hub for the Asian Bitcoin ecosystem. The Hong Kong Convention and Exhibition Centre is a world-class venue on the waterfront of Victoria Harbour — one of the most iconic event settings in Asia.

For attendees traveling from mainland China, Southeast Asia, Japan, Korea, and beyond, Hong Kong is one of the most accessible and well-connected cities in the region. The combination of a major Bitcoin event with Hong Kong's extraordinary food scene, cultural richness, and financial sophistication makes for a compelling trip.

The Bitcoin Asia Opportunity

Asia represents one of the fastest-growing segments of the global Bitcoin community. Countries like South Korea, Japan, Vietnam, Indonesia, and the Philippines have seen explosive growth in Bitcoin adoption, and events like Bitcoin Asia serve as the connective tissue for a community that is often underrepresented at the major US and European conferences.

For Western Bitcoiners, Bitcoin Asia is a rare opportunity to engage with the Asian side of the global Bitcoin community — to understand the different perspectives, use cases, and conversations that shape how Bitcoin is understood and used across the continent.

Planning Your Trip

  • Book early. Hong Kong hotels fill up fast around major events. The Convention Centre area has several excellent options within walking distance.
  • Extend your stay. Hong Kong rewards time. The food alone justifies a few extra days — from dim sum in Kowloon to rooftop bars on Hong Kong Island.
  • Visa check. Many nationalities can enter Hong Kong visa-free. Check requirements for your passport well in advance.
  • Connectivity. Hong Kong has excellent mobile data infrastructure. A local SIM or eSIM will keep you well-connected throughout your stay.
