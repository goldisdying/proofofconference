How to Use These Codes

Other Ways to Save on Bitcoin Conference Tickets

Discount codes aren't the only way to reduce costs. A few other strategies worth knowing:

  • Buy early. Almost every conference on this list offers early bird pricing that can save 30–50% compared to full price. The sooner you commit, the more you save.
  • Volunteer. Many events — including BTC Prague and BitBlockBoom — offer free or heavily discounted tickets in exchange for volunteering. Check the event websites for applications.
  • Watch for flash sales. Events often run 24–48 hour sales around Bitcoin milestones or key dates. Following @ProofOfConf on X is the fastest way to catch these.
  • Bundle travel. If you're attending BTC Prague and Freedom Tech Summit (June 10–13, same city), you only need one flight and hotel stay for two events.
