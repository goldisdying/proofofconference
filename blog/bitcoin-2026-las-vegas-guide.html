Confirmed Speakers

The 2026 lineup is already one of the strongest in the conference's history — featuring major names from Bitcoin, finance, and US government.

Michael Saylor — Strategy (MicroStrategy)

The most prominent corporate Bitcoin advocate in the world. Saylor's Strategy holds over 700,000 BTC, making it the largest publicly traded corporate Bitcoin holder. His appearances at Bitcoin conferences are consistently among the most watched content in the ecosystem.

Paul Atkins — SEC Chairman

The 34th Chairman of the Securities and Exchange Commission, appointed by President Trump and sworn in April 2025. Atkins is a pro-innovation regulator — his presence at Bitcoin 2026 signals a dramatically different regulatory environment than previous years. This is a significant get for the conference and for Bitcoin broadly.

Mike Selig — CFTC Chairman

The 16th Chairman of the Commodity Futures Trading Commission, also Trump-appointed. Having both the SEC and CFTC chairs at the same Bitcoin conference is unprecedented — it reflects how central Bitcoin has become to US financial policy conversations.

Afroman

The rapper behind "Because I Got High" brings a different energy to the main stage. Bitcoin's cultural tent is wide.

With 500+ speakers total, the full lineup covers founders, developers, miners, investors, policy makers, and cultural figures from across the global Bitcoin community. Check the full speakers list at 2026.b.tc.

Conference Tracks

Bitcoin 2026 runs six distinct content tracks across the three days:

  • Bitcoin 101 — fundamentals for newcomers and the Bitcoin-curious
  • AI + Compute + Mining — Bitcoin mining, energy, data centers, and AI infrastructure
  • Code & Country — regulation, nation-state adoption, and monetary policy
  • Culture — art, media, philosophy, and the social movements around Bitcoin
  • Open Source — Bitcoin development, protocol, and core contributors
  • Enterprise — treasury strategy, custody, payments, and institutional adoption

Whether you're a developer, investor, policy-watcher, or first-timer, there's a dedicated track for you.
