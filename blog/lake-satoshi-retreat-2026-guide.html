Lake Satoshi Retreat 2026: The Complete Guide

The Lake Satoshi Retreat 2026 returns to Michigan for its annual gathering — July 31 to August 2 in the Great Lakes region. A multi-day Bitcoin retreat combining lakeside community, education, and the extraordinary natural setting of Michigan's lake country.

More information at lakesatoshi.com.

The Basics

Dates: July 31–August 2, 2026
Location: Michigan, United States
Format: In-Person retreat

The Lake Satoshi Format

Like Camp Nakamoto in New Hampshire, Lake Satoshi Retreat deliberately steps away from the conference hall format. Michigan's lake country in late July and early August is extraordinary — warm, green, and alive with the energy of a Great Lakes summer. The retreat format allows for the kind of extended, genuine engagement that a 90-minute conference slot never can.

For Bitcoiners in the Midwest, Lake Satoshi Retreat is often the most personally meaningful event of the year — combining intellectual engagement with the kind of human connection that builds lasting community.

Michigan in Summer

Michigan has more freshwater coastline than any other state in the contiguous US. The lakes are warm in late July, the forests are lush, and the combination of water, woods, and small-town Michigan hospitality creates a setting that feels genuinely restorative. Detroit Metropolitan Airport and Chicago O'Hare both serve as convenient entry points for the region.
