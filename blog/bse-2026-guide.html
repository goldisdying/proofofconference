BSE: Bitcoin – Sovereignty – Economics 2026: The Complete Guide

BSE — Bitcoin, Sovereignty, Economics — 2026 takes place September 12 in Bury St Edmunds, one of England's most beautiful and historic market towns. A focused one-day conference with 175 attendees exploring the intersection of Bitcoin, personal sovereignty, and economic principles.

More information at bsebtc.co.uk.

The Basics

Date: September 12, 2026
Location: Bury St Edmunds, England
Expected Attendance: 175
Format: In-Person

Bitcoin, Sovereignty, Economics

The BSE framework — Bitcoin as a tool for personal sovereignty and sound economic principles — reflects a distinctly British tradition of thinking about money, freedom, and the relationship between individuals and institutions. The conference brings together Bitcoin advocates, economists, and sovereignty thinkers for a focused day of serious intellectual engagement.

Bury St Edmunds

Bury St Edmunds is a gem of a market town in Suffolk, East Anglia — one of England's best-preserved medieval town centers, with a magnificent Abbey ruins, a Georgian street grid, and some of the finest independent shops and restaurants in the region. It's about 1.5 hours from London Liverpool Street by train, making it easily accessible for a day trip from the capital or a longer stay in the Suffolk countryside.
