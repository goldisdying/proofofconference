How to Save on Bitcoin Conference Tickets

Bitcoin conferences aren't cheap. Here's how to keep costs down:

  • Use discount codes. Proof of Conference tracks every available promo code. The code FOMO works at several major events including Bitcoin Amsterdam.
  • Buy early. Most conferences offer significant early bird pricing — often 30–50% below door prices.
  • Volunteer. Many events accept volunteer applications in exchange for free or discounted admission.
  • Look for side events. Satellite events, happy hours, and community meetups around major conferences are often free and surprisingly valuable.
