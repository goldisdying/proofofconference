European Mining Summit 2026: The Complete Guide

The European Mining Summit 2026 takes place September 23 in Helsinki, Finland — the premier Bitcoin mining conference for the European continent. Confirmed speakers include Rachel Geyer, Robert Schulman, Jaakko Kaijala, Kasimir Kaitue, Martin Paasi, and Roni Karjalainen.

Tickets at miningsummit.eu.

The Basics

Date: September 23, 2026
Location: Helsinki, Finland
Format: In-Person

Confirmed Speakers

Rachel Geyer, Robert Schulman, Jaakko Kaijala, Kasimir Kaitue, Martin Paasi, Roni Karjalainen, and more to be announced.

Why Helsinki for European Mining

The Nordic countries have become increasingly important in global Bitcoin mining — cheap, renewable hydroelectric and wind power, cold climates that reduce cooling costs, and stable regulatory environments make Scandinavia and Finland natural hosts for mining operations. Finland in particular has a long history of technology and engineering excellence.

The European Mining Summit fills a genuine gap on the calendar — while Mining Disrupt (Miami) serves the US market, European miners and mining industry professionals have had fewer dedicated gathering points. Helsinki on September 23 also conveniently precedes Bitcoin Helsinki (September 25–26) just two days later — one trip to Finland, two strong events.
