What to Wear to a Bitcoin Conference: A Practical Guide

If you are attending your first Bitcoin conference, you have probably wondered what people actually wear. The short answer: Bitcoin merch. The conference floor at events like Bitcoin 2027, BTC Prague, and BitBlockBoom is a sea of black t-shirts, hoodies, and hats with orange Bitcoin logos. It is the unofficial uniform and the fastest way to signal that you belong.

Here is a practical guide to dressing for a Bitcoin conference, including the best places to find Bitcoin-only apparel, comfort tips for long days on the expo floor, and the one thing you should never wear.

The Unwritten Dress Code

Bitcoin conferences are casual. You will see everything from graphic tees to button-downs, but the overwhelming majority of attendees wear some form of Bitcoin-branded clothing. This is not a coincidence. Wearing Bitcoin merch serves a practical purpose: it is the easiest conversation starter in a room full of strangers. Someone sees your shirt, comments on it, and now you are talking.

The vibe varies slightly by event. The main Bitcoin Conference (Bitcoin 2026 in Las Vegas, Bitcoin 2027 in Nashville) skews a bit more polished on Pro/Industry Day, where you will see blazers alongside t-shirts. Smaller events like BitBlockBoom, Camp Nakamoto, and bitcoin++ are fully casual. BTC Prague leans European-casual. But at every single one, Bitcoin merch is the default.

Practical Tips for Conference Comfort

Wear comfortable shoes

This is the most important piece of advice in this entire guide. You will be on your feet for 8-12 hours a day walking an expo floor, standing in speaker lines, and moving between stages. Sneakers or comfortable walking shoes are the move. Leave the dress shoes at home.

Layer up

Convention centers blast the AC. The Venetian in Las Vegas, the Music City Center in Nashville, the O2 Universum in Prague all crank the air conditioning to counterbalance thousands of bodies in a closed space. Bring a hoodie or light jacket even if it is 95 degrees outside. A Bitcoin hoodie over a Bitcoin t-shirt is peak conference layering.

Think about your neckline

You will be wearing a lanyard with a badge for three days straight. Crew neck t-shirts work best. V-necks and wide collars let the lanyard slide around and look sloppy. Button-up shirts with an unbuttoned collar also work. The lanyard is part of the outfit whether you like it or not.

Bring a light bag or backpack

You will accumulate swag, stickers, business cards, and maybe a hardware wallet or two. A small backpack or crossbody bag keeps your hands free for handshakes, phone swaps, and food. Pockets alone will not cut it by Day 2.

Pack extra shirts

If you are attending a multi-day conference, bring a different Bitcoin shirt for each day. Wearing the same shirt three days in a row is noticed. Three different FOMO21 shirts across three days is a power move.

What NOT to Wear

Altcoin merch at a Bitcoin conference. This is the cardinal rule. Do not wear Ethereum, Solana, Cardano, or any other altcoin branding to a Bitcoin-only event. You will get heckled at best and shunned at worst. Bitcoin conferences are Bitcoin-only spaces, and showing up in altcoin gear is like wearing a Yankees jersey to Fenway Park. Some people will laugh. Some will genuinely not talk to you. It is not worth it.

A full suit. Unless you are a speaker on the Industry/Pro stage or walking into a whale-tier reception, a suit is overdressing. You will stand out for the wrong reasons. Business casual is the ceiling. A blazer over a Bitcoin tee is the sweet spot if you want to look sharp without trying too hard.

Sandals or open-toed shoes. The expo floor is crowded. People will step on your feet. Protect them.

The Bottom Line

Bitcoin conferences are one of the few professional settings where wearing a graphic t-shirt is not just acceptable but expected. Lean into it. Get quality Bitcoin merch, bring comfortable shoes, layer for the AC, and leave the altcoin logos at home. You will fit right in.
