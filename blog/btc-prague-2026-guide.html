BTC Prague 2026: The Complete Guide

BTC Prague 2026 is shaping up to be the biggest European Bitcoin conference ever held. Now in its fourth year, the event has grown from a scrappy community gathering into the continent's flagship Bitcoin-only conference — and 2026 is expected to draw over 10,000 attendees from across the globe.

Here's everything you need to know to plan your trip.

Why BTC Prague Stands Apart

BTC Prague has built a reputation for a few things the larger US events sometimes lack: a genuinely European perspective on Bitcoin, a walkable city that makes networking easy, and an atmosphere that feels more like a community celebration than a trade show. The venue at PVA Expo Praha is large enough to accommodate thousands of attendees while keeping sessions focused and accessible.

The 2026 speaker lineup includes Michael Saylor, Efrat Fenigson, Knut Svanholm, Giacomo Zucco, André Dragosch, Daniel Prince, and many more — a mix of global Bitcoin heavyweights and the distinctly European voices that make BTC Prague unique.

The Prague Advantage

Prague is one of the great Bitcoin cities in Europe. The city has a thriving Bitcoin circular economy, with many restaurants, hotels, and businesses accepting Bitcoin directly. It's also one of the most beautiful and affordable capital cities on the continent — excellent for extending your trip before or after the conference.

The city center is a short metro or taxi ride from PVA Expo Praha, and there are hotels at every price point within easy distance of both the venue and the historic center.

Add Freedom Tech Summit on June 10

If you're making the trip to Prague for BTC Prague, consider arriving a day early for Freedom Tech Summit on June 10 at Hotel Duo. It covers Bitcoin, Lightning, Nostr, Cashu, and privacy technology with a deep technical focus — the ideal warm-up to BTC Prague for developers and cypherpunks. One trip, two exceptional events.

Getting to Prague

Prague's Václav Havel Airport has direct flights from most major European cities and many international hubs. From the airport, the venue and city center are accessible by taxi, rideshare, or public transit. The city's metro system is clean, reliable, and easy to navigate.
