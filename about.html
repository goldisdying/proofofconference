What Is Proof of Conference?

Proof of Conference is the leading directory for Bitcoin conferences worldwide. We track every major Bitcoin event — dates, venues, speakers, ticket links, and verified discount codes — so that Bitcoiners can plan their conference year in one place.

The directory covers 60+ events across 30+ countries, from the flagship Bitcoin 2026 in Las Vegas and BTC Prague in Europe, to community gatherings in Australia, Latin America, Africa, and Asia. Every conference page includes event details, travel information, and discount codes where available.

Proof of Conference is independent — we are not affiliated with any conference organizer. Our goal is to give the global Bitcoin community a single trusted source for conference information.

Who Runs Proof of Conference?

Neil Jacobs

Proof of Conference was founded by Neil Jacobs — a Bitcoin brand and marketing strategist, global conference speaker, and founder of FOMO21 (Bitcoin apparel).

Neil has spoken at Bitcoin 2023, Bitcoin 2024, Bitcoin 2025, BTC Prague, and other major Bitcoin conferences worldwide. He has been active in the Bitcoin conference community for years and built Proof of Conference to solve a genuine problem: there was no single authoritative source tracking every Bitcoin conference globally.
